# Data for AI

An international community of engineers building data infrastructure for AI. We run practitioner-led meetups in San Francisco and across Asia.

## What we do

- **Meetups.** Engineers share how they run data and AI infrastructure in production: data agents, semantic layers, catalogs, lakehouses, and the platforms around them.
- **[Practices](https://github.com/dataforai/practices).** Full write-ups of meetup talks, plus articles by practitioners. Each covers what worked, what failed, and why.

## Get involved

- **Come to a meetup.** Upcoming events: [luma.com/dataforai](https://luma.com/dataforai)
- **Speak or write.** Propose a talk or an article by opening an issue in [community](https://github.com/dataforai/community/issues) with a title, a short summary and the language.
- **Contribute.** Fix a typo, improve a translation, add a missing link. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/dataforai/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

Everyone who takes part follows our [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/dataforai/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
