# Contributing

Thanks for helping. Anyone can open an issue or send a pull request to a public repository in this organization.

## How to contribute

1. For anything bigger than a typo, open an issue first and describe the change.
2. Send a pull request from your fork.
3. A maintainer reviews it. Small fixes are usually merged as they are.

## Ground rules

- **No secrets.** Never commit passwords, tokens or API keys.
- **No personal data.** Keep attendee lists and anyone's contact details out of the repositories.
- **No invite links or QR codes.** They expire and leave dead links behind.
- **Speakers' words stay theirs.** Keep talk titles, quotes and job titles as the speaker gave them. Put a translation next to the original, not in place of it.
- **Slides belong to speakers.** We publish a deck only when its speaker agrees.

By contributing, you agree to follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
